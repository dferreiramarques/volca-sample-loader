Connecting
Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (or Chrome on Android with a USB OTG cable) and allow MIDI with SysEx when asked. Connect the volca sample 2 with its micro-USB cable and press Connect. Safari and iPhone/iPad don't support Web MIDI.

            
Pads
Pads 1–10 play the volca's parts over MIDI so you can hear a sample after sending it. Part 1 listens on the channel below, part 2 on the next, and so on.

            
Good to know
Samples are stored as 16-bit mono at 31.25 kHz, the volca's own format. If the volca stops responding during a long transfer, switch it off and on: its memory stays intact. Long samples take a few seconds to travel over USB.
